# 🍎 NutriMind AI

NutriMind AI is a state-of-the-art mobile application designed to simplify and revolutionize your health and nutrition journey. Powered by advanced artificial intelligence, NutriMind AI empowers users to take control of their daily dietary habits, hydration levels, and wellness goals.

---

> [!NOTE]
> **Status:** 🚧 Under Active Development / قيد التطوير النشط 🚧

---

## ✨ Key Features (المميزات الرئيسية)

- **📸 AI Food Scanner (ماسح الوجبات الذكي)**
  Analyze meals instantly using your device's camera. Get real-time breakdowns of calorie counts, macronutrients (Protein, Carbs, Fats), and an overall health score.

- **💧 Smart Hydration Tracker (متابع شرب الماء الذكي)**
  Dynamic hydration assistant that calculates water targets based on your metrics and provides custom targets.

- **📊 Comprehensive Daily Summary (ملخص اليوم)**
  A polished visual dashboard detailing your calorie consumption, remaining targets, and detailed macro progress.

- **💬 Personal AI Nutritionist (مستشار التغذية الذكي)**
  An intelligent, interactive chat interface to answer your dietary questions, suggest recipes, and tailor your nutrition plan.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack (التقنيات المستخدمة)

- **Framework:** Flutter
- **Language:** Dart
- **State Management:** BLoC / Cubit
- **Backend:** Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Storage)
- **AI Integrations:** Gemini & Groq APIs
- **Localization:** easy_localization (Supports English & Arabic)
